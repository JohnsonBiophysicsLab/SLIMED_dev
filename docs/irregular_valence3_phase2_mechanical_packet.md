# Valence-3 OpenSubdiv Phase-2 Mechanical Packet

Date: 2026-08-03

Status: proof-only continuation; no production route or scientific baseline is
approved by this packet.

## Scope

This packet advances the first mechanical portion of Phase 2 from
`irregular_valence3_analysis_and_implementation_plan.md`. It uses the actual
OpenSubdiv 3.7.0 rows generated at adaptive isolation level 5 and the existing
SLIMED `Mesh::element_energy_force_regular()` algebra. It does not install a
default caller, mutate a production mesh, populate one-rings, or change CUDA.

The proof now checks:

1. per-sample and stacked `g dot (W p) == (W^T g) dot p` identities with
   deterministic asymmetric coordinates and gradients;
2. canonical preparation through `prepare_source_keyed_kernel_call()`;
3. direct accumulation versus production-shaped `sourceCount * 9` scatter;
4. ascending reductions with 1, 2, and 4 caller-owned buffers, repeated
   twice;
5. true net-force and net-torque residuals for bending, area, and volume; and
6. the Phase-1 finite-difference, normal, topology, provider, isolation-level,
   dependency-disabled, and then-observed legacy-volume mismatch contracts.

All comparisons are source-keyed. No local-slot padding or
`Face::oneRingVertices` data is introduced.

## Measured results

The following values came from the OpenSubdiv-enabled proof runner. They are
diagnostic observations and are not approved scientific baselines.

| Fixture | max transpose residual | max scatter/reduction residual | force balance |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| regular valence-3 tetrahedron | `4.30e-19` | `6.26e-15` | pass |
| asymmetric valence-3 tetrahedron | `4.88e-19` | `7.94e-15` | pass |
| closed mixed valence 3/4/5 | `3.16e-19` | `2.91e-16` | volume fails |

For the two exact tetrahedron cases, every net-force and net-torque relative
residual is below `2.80e-13`, satisfying the fixed mechanical proof threshold
of `5e-10`.

The mixed fixture remains outside the exact tetrahedron provider contract.
Its bending and area net-force residuals are below `7e-16`, but its volume
net-force residual is approximately `5.20e-3`. The harness now requires this
imbalance to remain visible as an unsupported mixed-dispatch diagnostic. It
does not widen the force-balance threshold or describe the mixed result as
production-valid physics.

This result is consistent with the architectural restriction already imposed
by Phase 1: rows generated by the general characterization helper are useful
for locating mixed 3/4/5 hazards, but only the exact oriented tetrahedron row
provider has reviewed Ptex/source identity and may enter the valence-3 proof
contract.

## Gate interpretation

The completed mechanical gates are:

- real-row per-sample and stacked transpose identity;
- canonical four-source mapping for the exact tetrahedron;
- force-family/source/axis-preserving scatter and deterministic reduction;
- true translation and rotation invariants for both exact tetrahedron
  coordinate sets; and
- explicit rejection and imbalance diagnosis for the non-applicable mixed
  topology.

The following Phase-2 work remains open:

- an independent long-double science oracle that does not call the SLIMED
  evaluator;
- all ten global and per-face energy channels;
- translation, rotation, and scale covariance runs;
- nested quadrature convergence beyond the current three-point plan;
- serial/OpenMP repeat matrices at fixed thread counts;
- output and V1/V2 checkpoint visibility; and
- an explicit scientific decision on the volume functional and accepted
  valence-3 integration plan. This historical Phase-2 blocker was subsequently
  resolved by selecting the full-divergence functional for the exact
  Valence-3 lane and proving its energy/force conjugacy.

Phase 3 remains blocked until those items are complete and a scientific
baseline is explicitly accepted. The current production-route and
default-caller values remain false.
